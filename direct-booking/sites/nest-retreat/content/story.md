For years, this spot on a quiet West Seattle street held nothing more than an old garage: a place for tools, bikes, and whatever didn't fit anywhere else. When it came time for something new, the footprint stayed, but the cottage built on it reached upward.

That's where the Nest gets its name. The main living space is perched above, where large windows frame the open sky and wood beams and warm wood accents wrap the room in a soft, sheltering glow. It feels a little like being tucked into a nest: cozy and protected, yet lifted toward the light, the clouds, and the ever-changing Seattle sky.

Below, the neighborhood is settled and quiet. Established homes, tended gardens, neighbors who wave. A few blocks away, the street gives way to the water, where the Sound stretches wide and the air smells like salt.

Your days here unfold slowly. Wake to soft light pouring through the windows, wander down to the beach with a coffee, and come home to your perch as the sky turns gold. Watch the weather roll in, or the stars come out, from a spot that feels just a little closer to both.

A small home with a big view of the sky. Settle in and stay a while.
