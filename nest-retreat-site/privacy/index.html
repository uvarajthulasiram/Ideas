Privacy

Draft: this page is still being reviewed and may change before bookings open.

This page explains what we collect when you book The Nest Retreat on this website, and why.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and phone number, and any note you leave for your host.
  • Your booking: dates, number of guests, the price and what you've paid.

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies, and the site loads no third-party tracking.

What we don't collect

Your card details go straight to Stripe, our payment processor. We never see or store your card number.

Why we use it

  • To take and manage your booking, and to charge any balance you owe.
  • To send your confirmation, arrival details and anything urgent during your stay.
  • To keep the records the law requires, such as for taxes.

Who else handles it

  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and its booking system, sends our emails, and runs a bot check (Turnstile) on the booking form to stop automated abuse.

We don't sell your information or share it for marketing.
