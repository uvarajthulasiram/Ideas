<h2>Refunds</h2>
<p>How much is refunded depends on how far ahead you cancel, counted from your check-in date:</p>
<ul><li>At least 30 days before check-in: a full refund of what you've paid</li><li>At least 14 days before check-in: a 50% refund of what you've paid</li><li>Fewer than 14 days before check-in: no refund of what you've paid</li></ul>

<p>Refunds go back to the card you paid with. Banks usually show them within 5 to 10 business days.</p>
<h2>How to cancel</h2>
<p>Email hello@example.com with your booking reference (it's in your confirmation email). The date we receive your email is the date that counts.</p>

<p>You pay 50% when you book and the rest 30 days before you arrive. Arriving within 30 days? You pay in full when you book. If you cancel before the balance is charged, the balance is never taken, and the refund applies to what you've paid so far.</p>
