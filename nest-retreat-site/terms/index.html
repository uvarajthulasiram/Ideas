Booking terms

Draft: this page is still being reviewed and may change before bookings open.

These terms apply to stays at The Nest Retreat booked on this website. "We" means Allie, the host. "You" means the guest who makes the booking.

Your booking

  • Your booking is confirmed when payment succeeds and you receive our confirmation email.
  • While you pay, your dates are held for 30 minutes. If payment isn't completed in that time, the dates are released.
  • Prices are in USD and include the taxes shown at checkout. The price you see before paying is the price you pay.

Payment

  • You pay 50% when you book and the rest 30 days before you arrive. Arriving within 30 days? You pay in full when you book.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. When you pay a deposit, your card is saved with Stripe so the balance can be charged on the due date. We never see or store your card number.
