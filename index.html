What changed for hosts

The platform now decides what you can ask of your guests.

Airbnb announced a new house rules policy on October 20, 2026. Here is what hosts are adjusting to.

7

Standard house rules

Hosts choose from seven set rules: guest limit, quiet hours, events, pets, smoking, commercial photography and lock-up at checkout.

Checkout chores limited

No more asking guests to wash dishes, strip beds, start laundry or take trash off-site. Light requests like dishes in the sink are still fine.

Contracts restricted

Separate guest agreements are allowed only for a few reasons, such as legal requirements, HOA rules or monthly stays.

Listings can be flagged

Unresolved requests can trigger an alert on your listing, like "includes cleaning requests". No extra charges for skipped chores.

15.5%

Host-only service fee

The split fee is gone. Most hosts now carry the whole platform fee on every booking.

Sources: Airbnb Resource Centre, "Important changes to house rules" (Oct 20, 2026); Airbnb host fee announcements. Details may change; check the platform's current policy.
